# 彼氏マウント Scene 1 — カメラの文法を入れた版（v1との差分例）

v1の書き方：「medium two-shot」「Cut to Ayaka's medium close-up」「Cut to Rena's close-up」
→ 横並び・全部アイレベル・肩越しなし・寄りの動機なし・リアクションなし。

カメラ文法版：
```
[Stage 1 | 0-4s]
Shot 1: medium two-shot with depth, Rena nearer on the left (her shoulder large in the left foreground), Ayaka across the table on the right | eye level, 45-degree | none | handheld hold with micro-shake, a quick reframe toward the phone as it comes up | Rena looks screen-right at Ayaka | shallow depth, the city window soft.
Shot 2 (cut while Rena's second line is still playing): close-up of Ayaka, face filling 55% of the frame height | low angle looking up at her | over Rena's blurred hair on the left edge | handheld hold | Ayaka's eyes on Rena, unimpressed, one slow blink | shallow.

[Stage 2 | 4-7s]
Insert (0.6s): extreme close-up of the orange handbag landing on the white tablecloth, gold lock bouncing once, teacup rattling | top-down | none | camera shudders on the thud.
Shot 3: close-up of Ayaka | low angle | over Rena's shoulder on the left edge | slow push-in that starts on 「で」 and stops on 「は」 | Ayaka looks screen-left at Rena, head perfectly still | shallow.

[Stage 3 | 7-10s]
Shot 4: extreme close-up of Rena, top of her head cropped, eyes and mouth only | slightly high angle looking down at her | Ayaka's dark hair soft on the right edge | handheld hold, then a tiny drift as her smile dies | Rena's eyes flick down to her own phone, then up | shallow.
```
